**Forest Trust** and **External Trust**:

**# Forest Trust:** this trust is wide and applied to all domains in the forest, this can only be configured from the root domain of the forest. 

**# External Trust:** This trust is only between 2 domains and only these two domains can use this trust. Like for example a domain **star.com** and us**.nav.com**, only these 2 domains use this trust.   

___

Types of Trusts, **Two Way**, **One Way** and **Transitive Trust**.

___

- **One Way Trust:** This trust is only in 1 direction like, one domain becomes trusting domain and one domain will be trusted domain
