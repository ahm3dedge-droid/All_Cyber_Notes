- Covers solids, liquids, gases, and everything in between

## Dimensions (Three Fundamental)

- **T** - Time (seconds)
- **L** - Length (meters)
- **M** - Mass (kilograms)

_All physical quantities can be expressed in terms of these three_

**Examples:**

- Area = L²
- Density = M/L³
- Speed = L/T
- Frequency = 1/T
- Angle = dimensionless

## Dimensional Analysis

**Critical tool to check work!**

Rules:

- ✓ Multiply/divide quantities of ANY dimension
- ✓ Add/subtract quantities of SAME dimension only
- Treat M, L, T algebraically

**If left side ≠ right side dimensions → ERROR!**

---

## Significant Figures

Use **only reliably known digits**

|Operation|Rule|
|---|---|
|Addition/Subtraction|Round to least decimal places|
|Multiplication/Division|Round to least significant figures|

Examples:

- 95.9 + 39.32 = 135.22 → **135**
- 95.9 - 39.32 = 56.58 → **56.6**
- (105.8)(31.4) = 3322.12 → **3320**
- (105.8)/(31.4) = 3.36943 → **3.37**
