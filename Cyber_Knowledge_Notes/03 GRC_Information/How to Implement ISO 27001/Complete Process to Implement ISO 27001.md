## How to Implement ISO 27001?
### Step 1: Perform Gap Assessment and identify Gaps.
**We need to identify:**
1. where do we stand today?
2. the current practices
3. The technologies used for business processes.
4. current documented policies
5. the *organizational structure*
6. *roles responsibilities*
> Now in comparison to the Requirements of ISO 27001 Standard, we check what requirements are missing and not implemented in our organization.
> We document those missing things and this will be the output and result of the *Gap assessment*. 

**Identified Gaps can be: 🔍**
- Missing **Vendor Risk Assessments**.
- Missing **Asset Inventory**.
- Missing **Access Rights Reviews**.
- Missing **Periodic Recovery Drills**. 
- Missing **Business Impact Analysis**.
- Missing **Physical Security Controls**. 
- Missing **Patch Management**.
- Missing **Malware Protection Solutions**.

#### Deliverables of the Gap Assessment will be 📄
- Gap Assessment Sheet, with graphs and tables, with all ISO 27001 controls and identified Gaps, with recommendations.

### Step 2: Perform Risk Assessment.
2 types of risk assessments:
**1. Asset based risk assessment:**
in this RA we identify assets, their value an organization, and based on this *asset value* we do analysis of risks, and how much the risk will impact if exploited.

**2. Directly start straightaway by Risks:**
in this RA type, we just generally starts from generic risks, and expand our Risk register to the more and more risks to organization.

#### Deliverables of the RIsk Assessment will be 📄
a professionally created and custom designed tailored to the organization's need, Excel File formally called as a **Risk Register**. 

Below is the list of columns should be defined in the Risk Register:
### **Risk Register – Columns (MD Table)**

| Column Name                 | Short Description                                                                    |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Risk ID                     | Unique identifier used to track the risk                                             |
| Risk Title / Risk Name      | Short and clear name of the risk                                                     |
| Risk Description            | Detailed explanation of the risk scenario                                            |
| Risk Category               | Classification of the risk (e.g., Strategic, Operational, Cybersecurity, Compliance) |
| Cause (Root Cause)          | Underlying reason why the risk exists                                                |
| Impact Description          | Description of potential consequences if the risk occurs                             |
| Likelihood (Probability)    | Chance of the risk occurring (Low/Medium/High or numeric scale)                      |
| Impact Rating               | Severity of impact if the risk materializes                                          |
| Risk Rating / Risk Score    | Calculated value based on likelihood and impact                                      |
| Risk Level                  | Overall risk classification (Low, Medium, High, Critical)                            |
| Risk Owner                  | Individual or role accountable for the risk                                          |
| Existing Controls           | Current controls or safeguards in place                                              |
| Control Effectiveness       | Assessment of how effective the existing controls are                                |
| Inherent Risk Rating        | Risk level before applying any controls                                              |
| Residual Risk Rating        | Remaining risk level after controls are applied                                      |
| Risk Treatment Option       | Chosen response (Avoid, Mitigate, Transfer, Accept)                                  |
| Mitigation Actions          | Planned actions to reduce or manage the risk                                         |
| Action Owner                | Person responsible for executing mitigation actions                                  |
| Target Completion Date      | Planned date to complete mitigation actions                                          |
| Risk Status                 | Current state of the risk (Open, In Progress, Closed)                                |
| Review Date                 | Date when the risk was last reviewed                                                 |
| Next Review Date            | Scheduled date for the next risk review                                              |
| Residual Risk Acceptance    | Indicates whether residual risk is formally accepted                                 |
| Approval Authority          | Management authority approving risk acceptance                                       |
| Related Policy / Control ID | Reference to applicable policies or control frameworks                               |
| Affected Assets / Systems   | Systems, applications, or data impacted by the risk                                  |
| Risk Trend                  | Direction of risk over time (Increasing, Decreasing, Stable)                         |
| Comments / Remarks          | Additional notes, observations, or updates                                           |

### Step 3: Create a risk Treatment Plan to treat identified risks
now we will create a risk treatment plan, we will use following list of risk treatment options
1. **Accept** the risk (and deal with the consequences)
2. **Reduce** or Mitigate the risk (take action to lower it)
3. **Transfer** the risk (for example, through insurance)
4. **Avoid** the risk completely

### Step 4: ISO 27001 is implemented now.
Now we can move forward to the Audit and Certification process. --->
