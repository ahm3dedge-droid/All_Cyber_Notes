# IMAP
port = 143 (non-secure)
port = 993 (secure SSL/TLS)

### 💡IMAP and POP3 will runs on the inbound email servers 
---
#### Definition
it is used to receive emails, it is used by the email clients to download emails from the email server, and show emails in our GMAIL inbox.
> NOTE: IMAP not only downloads the inbox emails, but also syncs all the other folders also, *spam*, *drafts*, *sent items*, *deleted*, etc.

- **easy to remember is IMAP just maps the whole structure**

#### Definition
this POP3 is also used to receive emails. 
> Note: POP3 only downloads the inbox emails to your web client.
