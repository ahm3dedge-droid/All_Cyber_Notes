## Network Communication Modes (Simplex, Half-Duplex, Full-Duplex)
![[Pasted image 20260116122048.png]]

#### 1. Simplex
simplex me sirf A --> B communication possible hai
B --> A koi communication possible ni hai

#### 2. Half-Duplex
half-duplex me ek time par, sirf A --> B ya B --> A communication kar skte hain 

#### 3. Full Duplex
full-duplex me same time par A --> B ya B --> A communication possible hai
