DLP (Data Loss Prevention) solutions are ==cybersecurity tools that prevent sensitive data from being lost, leaked, or misused by detecting, monitoring, and blocking unauthorized sharing or movement of information, protecting data at rest, in use, and in motion across endpoints, networks, and cloud apps to meet compliance (like GDPR, HIPAA) and stop insider threats or accidental exposure==. 

They use AI, ML, and policy enforcement to identify data (like PII, IP) and apply controls, ensuring data security for hybrid workforces and cloud environments
