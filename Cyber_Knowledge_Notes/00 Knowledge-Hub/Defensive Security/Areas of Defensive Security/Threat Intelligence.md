### Threat Intelligence
**Threat intelligence** is the process of collecting and analyzing information about **current and potential adversaries** to enable a **threat-informed defense**.

#### Core Concepts
- **Threat**: Any action that can harm systems or operations 
- **Adversaries** vary by industry and target:
    - Nation-state actors (political motives)
    - Cybercriminals / ransomware groups (financial motives)

### Threat Intelligence Lifecycle

1. **Data Collection**
    - *Internal sources:* system logs, network logs
    - *External sources:* forums, public reports, threat feeds
        
2. **Processing**
    - Normalize and organize raw data
        
3. **Analysis**
    - Identify attacker motives, capabilities, and targets
    - Extract actionable intelligence
        
4. **Action**
    - Predict attacker behavior
    - Improve detection, prevention, and response strategies

### Outcomes of Threat Intel
- Identification of **Threat Actor**
- Understanding of **Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs)**
- Improved preparedness and mitigation
