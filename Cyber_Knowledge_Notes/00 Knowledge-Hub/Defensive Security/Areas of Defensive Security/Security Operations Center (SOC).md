### Security Operations Center (SOC)

A _Security Operations Center_ (SOC) is a team of cyber security professionals that monitors the network and its systems to detect malicious cyber security events. Some of the main areas of interest for a SOC are:

**Vulnerabilities**
- Identify system weaknesses
- Apply patches or compensating controls
- Remediation may be handled outside SOC, but detection is critical

**Policy Violations**
- Detect violations of organizational security policies
	Example: uploading confidential data to unauthorized platforms

**Unauthorized Activity**
- Detect misuse of valid credentials (account compromise)
- Rapid detection prevents lateral movement and data loss

**Network Intrusions**
- Detect breaches via phishing, malware, or exploited services
- Early detection limits impact
